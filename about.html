ABOUT PESOWISE

Money guidance for everyday life.

PesoWise is a practical money-education website for readers in the Philippines. We make everyday topics—like budgeting, saving, and exploring side-income ideas—easier to understand and act on.

What we aim to do

Our goal is to explain useful money habits in clear, approachable language. Our guides focus on steps readers can consider in their own circumstances, rather than promises of quick wealth or guaranteed results.

Our topics

  • Practical budgeting and spending plans
  • Emergency savings and everyday saving habits
  • Responsible freelancing and side-income ideas
  • Digital products and small online projects
  • Simple tools that help readers plan and estimate

How we approach content

We aim to make articles clear, useful, and distinct. When a topic depends on changing rules, rates, fees, platform terms, or eligibility, readers should check the relevant official source before acting. We do not claim that every strategy is suitable for every person.

Where a page includes affiliate or sponsored material, we intend to identify that relationship clearly. Editorial content should not be treated as individualized financial, tax, legal, or investment advice.

Corrections and questions

If you spot an error or have a question about a published guide, please visit our Contact page.
