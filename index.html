01 / BUDGETING

The simple 50/30/20 budget

As a starting point, try assigning 50% of take-home pay to needs, 30% to wants, and 20% to savings or debt repayment. Your real-life split may need to look different — especially if housing or family costs are high. Track spending for a month, choose realistic categories, and adjust rather than giving up.

  • List your take-home income.
  • Write down fixed bills and everyday needs.
  • Set a small automatic savings amount if possible.
  • Review your plan each payday.
02 / SAVING

Start an emergency fund gently

Begin with a first milestone you can reach, such as ₱1,000 or one week's essential expenses. Keep emergency savings somewhere accessible and separate from everyday spending. Build toward a few months of essential costs as your situation allows.

  • Pick a starter goal.
  • Save a fixed amount on payday.
  • Use it for genuine urgent needs.
  • Replenish it after you use it.
